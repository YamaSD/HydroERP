FAQs

Frequently asked questions

1. What is Hydro ERP?

Hydro ERP is an all-in-one platform designed to simplify and manage hydropower projects, operations, resources, and business processes.

Who can use Hydro ERP?

Project managers, site engineers, store & inventory teams, plant operators and management teams at construction and hydropower companies across Nepal.

What can I manage with Hydro ERP?

DPRs, approvals, inventory, equipment, tools & custody, reports and insights — all from one connected platform.

Can I access Hydro ERP remotely?

Yes. HydroERP works on web and mobile, online or offline, so you can access it from the office, the site, or anywhere in between.
